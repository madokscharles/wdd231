KONOMA SITE PLAN

Author: Madokuboba Charles Dokubo

Site Name

Konoma

Konoma is a modern platform designed to connect people with trusted skilled professionals within their local communities. The name Konoma comes from my local language, Kalabari, where it means “Work.” It was chosen because it is simple, memorable, and represents connection, innovation, and the growth of local talent and opportunities.

Site Purpose

Konoma provides a searchable directory where users can find skilled professionals such as mechanics, electricians, tutors, cleaners, and designers. Users can browse categories, view talent profiles, check reviews, and contact service providers easily. The goal is to make finding reliable local help simple and safe.

Scenarios

Where can I quickly find a trusted plumber or electrician near my new area?

How can I compare different service providers using ratings and pricing?

Can I browse categories to find tutors, beauty services, or tech support?
